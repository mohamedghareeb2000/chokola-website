# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Chokola primarily serves young people, followed by families, who want a welcoming place to sit, enjoy premium desserts, and have drinks. The website addresses customers across the United States while giving natural priority to people who live near or plan to visit the physical branch.

## Product Purpose

The website exists to build awareness of the Chokola brand, showcase its menu and products, encourage branch visits, and support contact and inquiries—in that order. It presents Chokola as a destination and hospitality experience rather than an e-commerce storefront; online ordering is outside its scope.

## Brand Personality

Premium, indulgent, and warm. The experience should make desserts feel desirable and carefully crafted while keeping the lounge approachable, social, and inviting rather than distant or ceremonial.

## Anti-references

Chokola must not resemble childish candy branding, a generic bakery template, cold or overly formal luxury, excessively minimal layouts dominated by empty space, generic SaaS interfaces, or cluttered experiences with excessive animation. The supplied section references are collective visual direction for composition, spacing, product presentation, imagery, and interaction; no single reference should be copied literally.

## Design Principles

1. Make the desserts irresistible: let strong product imagery and presentation carry the appetite appeal.
2. Balance aspiration with hospitality: communicate premium quality without losing warmth or approachability.
3. Sell the destination, not a transaction: guide visitors from brand discovery to menu exploration, branch intent, and contact.
4. Compose with purpose: use the supplied references collectively to create varied, well-paced sections instead of repeating a template.
5. Preserve Chokola's identity: evolve the existing visual language rather than replacing it with borrowed aesthetics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain accessible text and control contrast, full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and interactions whose meaning does not depend on color alone. Keep these accommodations consistent with the existing brand identity.
